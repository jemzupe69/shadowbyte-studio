Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

ShadowByte Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using our Discord bots, digital products and development services, including ShadowByte Market.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the services you use, we may process:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames.
  • Discord server IDs and relevant server information.
  • Messages and information submitted through support tickets.
  • Order numbers, purchased products and order statuses.
  • Payment amounts, currencies and transaction identifiers.
  • Public blockchain transaction information when cryptocurrency payments are used.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and deliver customer orders.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Manage tickets and complaints.
  • Verify payment transactions.
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and improve our services.

4. Cryptocurrency Payments

When cryptocurrency payments are supported, transaction information may be processed to verify payments. Blockchain transactions are generally public and may be visible through public blockchain explorers.

We do not request or need your cryptocurrency wallet passwords, private keys or recovery phrases. Never share these details with us.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our services, process payments, provide support or comply with applicable laws.

Discord and blockchain networks operate independently and are subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain order records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, it should be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Submit a complaint to a competent data protection authority.

9. Third-Party Services

Our services may interact with Discord, cryptocurrency networks and other external platforms. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The latest version will always be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact ShadowByte Studio through our official Discord server.
